# Wellington Running Clubs — Comparison Report

**Perspective:** Social runner, new to running (can complete a slow 5 km), based in Wellington. Looking for social runs, confidence-building, and support.

**Clubs analysed:** Hutt Valley Harriers, Olympic Harriers, Trentham United, Wellington Harrier Athletic Club (WHAC), Wellington Scottish.

**Analysis date:** 23 June 2026.

**Method:** Website review plus public social/news channels discoverable from each site (Facebook, Instagram, Strava, weekly news/blog). Facebook and Instagram post feeds could not be fully reviewed without login; where noted, social-media conclusions are based on embedded feeds, site-linked channels, or publicly indexed weekly news.

**Limitations:** Membership costs for WHAC are not published on their website. Exact weekly venues for Wellington Scottish sessions vary by week and are primarily published via their app/Facebook group rather than a fixed public schedule.

---

## Executive summary

For a nervous beginner wanting social support in Wellington, **Wellington Scottish** and **Hutt Valley Harriers** stand out as the strongest fits. Scottish explicitly welcomes “beginners to elite,” offers a dedicated non-racing membership ($115), and structures Saturday sessions around pack jogs, a walking group, a social non-running option, and café visits — signals that map closely to your goals. HVH is the most affordable social option ($60), uses consistently warm “just come along” language, and makes it easy to try a Saturday without registering first — though you would need to travel to Petone.

**Olympic Harriers** and **Trentham United** are solid **Maybe** options: both offer explicit social/recreational membership tiers and multiple pace groups, but their online presence skews more toward families, juniors, and race results. **WHAC** has a welcoming “turn up and introduce yourself” message via third-party listings and hosts community-friendly events like Honest 10, but its website and newsletters read more performance- and prestige-oriented; the empty Join page and lack of published fees would make me hesitant as a first-time joiner turning up alone.

### What to expect on a first visit

- Harrier clubs often mix **racing weekends** with **social pack-run weekends** — always ask which kind you're walking into.
- Winter (Apr–Oct) is the main club season; you're arriving mid-season, which is fine — all clubs say newcomers are welcome during the season.
- A slow 5 km is enough to participate in social packs; many clubs also have **walkers**.
- You do not need club kit on day one.
