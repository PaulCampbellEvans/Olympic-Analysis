Get Involved

Come along and get to know us. There are loads of opportunities. Activities are geared for members, but everyone is welcome to come along for a taster. Whether you are looking for a community to help you be active or support to get your next personal best we have something for you.

Club Pack Runs (and social gathering) 

Club pack runs (and social gathering)
When Saturday afternoons (2:00pm) – when we don't have races. Keep an eye on the calendar.
Who Member-focused
What

We have a number of packs (running groups) to cater for different speeds, walkers, genders and ages. The duration of the runs and walks is roughly an hour. We have a clear "no one left behind policy". 

Winter – We meet at our club house just before 2pm on non-race weekends. Keep your eye on the calendar.

Summer – We have a club run every weekend, alternating between the clubrooms and members’ places. Keep your eye on the calendar.

Clothes – We run in all weather and many of our runs are in the hills around Johnsonville. Be prepared and bring a thermal top, windbreaker, hat and gloves if the weather is bad.

Social – Afterwards we gather at the club house to catch up over a drink and snacks. This is a great space to get to know the people behind the club. 
Where Club house, Waiora Hub Sports and Community Centre, Alex Moore Park, Johnsonville
Cost Covered by membership subs
Contact Show up and introduce yourself. 

 

Club Drills & Intervals

Club drills and intervals
When Tuesday evenings (6:00pm in the winter and 6:30 in the summer)   
Who Member-focused
What

Develop your technique. Sharpen your speed. Build your aerobic engine. Have fun doing it. 

The coach takes us through drills to develop our running technique before moving on to an interval session. The intervals are time-based (as opposed to distance-based) in order to accommodate everyone. There standard guide is push as hard as you feel comfortable with. 

Week on week the coach cycles through different interval sessions varying between threshhold, aerobic/VO2Max and anaerobic/speed to help us adapt. We do like to mix it up. 
Where Clubrooms, Waiora Hub Sports and Community Centre, Alex Moore Park, Johnsonville
Cost Covered by membership subs
Contact To find out more, contact our coaching coordinator through our contact us form.

Junior Middle Distance Training

Junior middle distance training
When Tuesday & Thursday afternoons (4:15pm–5pm)
Who Competitive juniors
What

The Junior Middle Distance programme provides specialised coaching for talented young athletes (year 5 through to college age) seeking to develop their competitive middle distance running. This stream is designed for committed athletes ready to progress their performance. 

Primary/Intermediate Squad – for kids who have been running competitions for several years.

College Squad – college aged (and older) who are competitive and looking at taking their running to the next level.
Where Nairnville Park, Khandallah
Contact To express interest, contact our coaching coordinator through our contact us form.

Junior Athletics Club Nights

Junior athletics club nights
When Wednesday nights (5:30pm–6:30pm). Summer only. Keep an eye on the calendar.
Who Primary school children
What

In summer, children from age 5 to 14 can participate in our children’s athletics programme to help develop their running, jumping and throwing skills.

3–6 year olds – we run the Get Set Go programme for 3 to 6 year olds. Get Set Go is an Athletics NZ programme aimed at Under 7s.  Last summer we had over 50 kids taking part each week.  There are a whole variety of games for the kids and their parents to have fun with based around running, jumping and throwing activities. A range of activities are set up each club night that the children rotate around.  Parents can either move around the activities with their kids, or volunteer to help run a station.  The kids are on the go the whole time – no long lines!  Get Set Go is a fantastic introduction to athletics for this age group. The Get Set Go programme is held on the bottom field at Grenada North Park so you can park on Caribbean Drive or off Nassau Street.

7–12 year olds – children rotate around coaching/activity stations.  Each club night finishes with a running race whether it's 60m, 80m, 100m or once around the track.

Sausage Sizzle – we  finish our club nights with a BBQ and some treats – a good way to feed the kids on the way home. We will be brings some BBQ and lollies down to the bottom field for the younger ones.
Where Middle Field, Grenada North Park, Grenada North
Cost Separate membership – see Join Us
Contact To express interest, contact our Juniors coordinator through our contact us form.

Core Strengthening Classes

Core strengthening classes
When Thursday evenings (7:30pm–8:30pm)
Who Anyone – not restricted to members
What

Core strength is an important aspect of injury prevention in running. 

The classes are tailored for the needs of runners and walkers, providing balanced strength of your running muscles. Whatever your own personal starting point, these classes will help you run faster, more efficiently and will protect against injuries.
Where Clubrooms, Waiora Hub Sports and Community Centre, Alex Moore Park, Johnsonville
Cost $42 per six week course
Contact Mark Anderson

Long Sunday Runs

Long Sunday runs
When Sunday mornings (8am-ish) Check the dedicated Long Sunday Runs Facebook Messenger group chat.
Who Member-focused
What

It's so much more fun to get your long mileage in with company. Join us on Sunday mornings. These usually start at 8:00am and are typically between 90min and 120min duration. This is a "roving" pack – meaning that our starting point and course vary from week-to-week – making it a great way to learn routes around Wellington. 

There are no requirements for speed, racing experience, or what distance means "long" for you, other than the agreed route that most of the group will run on a given Sunday. 

We are trying to grow the group to support diverse pace goals. For now it's best to engage with the Facebook Messenger group so that we can work with you. The more people to engage the greater the chance there will be a group that suits your pace needs
Where Various routes with various starting points – varies week-on-week.
Cost N/A
Contact Ask to join the Long Sunday Runs Facebook Messenger group chat.

Local Races

Local races
When Through the year. Exact dates vary. Keep an eye on the calendar.
Who Member-focused
What

We do love a bit of friendly competition. Racing is a great way to challenge yourself, and serves as a motivator to do that training. 

As an Athletics Wellington and Athletics New Zealand affiliated club we are actively involved in the local (interclub) and national race scene. There is a race roughly every second weekend; some are just for fun and some are all about championships, with everything in between.

Winter (Harrier) Season – focuses on cross country, mountain running and road. Most events have races for all age grades. Athletics Wellington runs a Winter Series points system to keep all the races interesting. 

Summer (Track) Season – focuses on Track & Field. Generally there are discrete events for juniors, and for seniors & masters. 

These are explored in more detail on our annual events page. 

In addition there are many local, national (and indeed international) events that are open to the general public. Olympic Harriers members, as the enthusiasts that we are, take part in many of these.  
Where Mainly local (Wellington metro area)
Cost Winter events are usually covered by your club subs while track events usually require a small extra payment. Check the individual events/race page. 
Contact Coordination done on a race-by-race basis - join our weekly email list or the Members Facebook Messenger group to stay informed.

Club Trips (formal)

Club trips (formal)
When July & October. Exact dates vary.
Who Member-focused
What

Olympic Harriers prioritises two formal club trips each year - the North Island Cross Country Champs in Taupō and the New Zealand Road Relay Champs (location varies). Both trips are partially subsidised by the club. We love to arrive at both events "in force". 

The North Island Cross Country Champs are held in Thermal Spa Park, usually the first weekend in July. 

The New Zealand Road Relay Champs the first weekend in October is the climax of the winter season, with clubs going head to head with their best relay teams. 

Find out more on the club events page. 
Where Taupō (North Island Cross Country Champs) and Varies (National Road Relay Champs) 
Cost Race fees are covered by member subs. We apply for grants to help subsidise costs. 
Contact Coordination done on a race-by-race basis – join the Members Facebook Messenger group to stay informed.
