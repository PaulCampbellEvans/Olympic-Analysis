Proposed site navigation

A newcomer-first information architecture for Olympic Harriers, implementing recommendations M1–M4, Q1–Q3, and L3 from olympic_recommendations.md. Hover the navigation bar above to explore dropdowns. The tree below is the full proposed sitemap.

New page or section
Renamed (clearer label or URL)
Consolidated / merged content
Retired — redirect elsewhere
Design principle: The live site hides joining under Membership → Joining and spreads schedule, fees, and contacts across four sections. The proposed tree puts every pre-visit answer under New here (first nav item, red button), then separates Join (money & registration) from Training (when & how we run). Events serves racers without dominating the homepage.
