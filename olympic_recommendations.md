# Olympic Harriers — New-Member Accessibility Recommendations

**Target club:** Olympic Harriers — [olympicharriers.nz](https://www.olympicharriers.nz/)  
**Based on:** `comparison_report.md` (23 June 2026) + live site review (23 June 2026)  
**Persona:** Social runner, new to running (slow 5 km), Wellington region, nervous about turning up alone

**Live-site note since comparison report:** A [Joining page](https://www.olympicharriers.nz/membership/joining/) exists (last updated 7 Apr 2024) with clear trial-run guidance (*“come along to a few Saturday afternoon club runs”*). It is not surfaced on the homepage and was easy to miss in the earlier analysis. Recommendations below treat this as **good content that needs promotion**, not content that needs to be written from scratch.

**Social media limitation:** Facebook, Instagram, and X feeds could not be fully reviewed (login required). Social recommendations are inferred from site references and comparison-report context; post frequency and comment engagement should be verified by the communications volunteer.

---

## Executive summary

Olympic Harriers’ biggest barrier for a nervous social beginner is not what the club offers in person — recreational membership at **$82**, multiple pace packs, walkers, and a “try a few Saturdays first” policy are all strong — but **how invisible that welcome is online**. The homepage leads with news, results, and race calendars; a newcomer must dig through Membership → Joining to learn they can simply show up, and nowhere on the site answers the three questions that matter most: *Which Saturday should I come? Where exactly do I stand? Who do I talk to?*

The highest-leverage fix is a **single “New to Olympic?” entry point** on the homepage (linking to or containing the joining-page content), with a pinned “next club run” callout from the calendar, Club Captain Rob McCrudden named as the first-contact, and plain language for slow joggers and walkers. That closes most of the gap with Hutt Valley Harriers and Wellington Scottish without a website rebuild.

### Inferences

- The club is likely **welcoming in person** (joining page, pack structure, family culture) but **presents as a race club online** — similar to the comparison report’s WHAC pattern, though less extreme.
- A beginner who lands on the homepage during a race-results-heavy week may assume every Saturday is a competitive event elsewhere in the region.
- Tuesday night training is mentioned for social members on the subs page, but time, location, and suitability for a slow 5 km runner are not stated anywhere found.

## Strengths to preserve

1. **Recreational membership tier ($82)** — competitively priced vs Wellington Scottish ($115) and Trentham ($90); clearly distinct from competitive subs on the subscriptions page. Do not bury this behind competitive pricing tables.

2. **Pack-run model** — About page explicitly mentions groups for different speeds, walkers, women, and juniors. This is the core reassurance for nervous newcomers; it should become the headline message, not a paragraph on an inner page.

3. **“Try before you join” culture** — Joining page already invites people to attend several Saturdays before registering. This matches Trentham’s trial policy and HVH’s “just come along” approach; it should be on the homepage.

4. **Infrastructure and volunteer depth** — Waiora Hub clubrooms, named captain/deputy captain, newsletter, Facebook groups, iCal feed, and active calendar. The plumbing exists; the **signposting** for newcomers does not.

## Suggested “New member” page outline

Use as wireframe for `/membership/new-members/` or expanded `/membership/joining/`:

### 1. Who we are (one paragraph)
Olympic Harriers is a Johnsonville-based running and walking club for all ages and abilities — from social joggers and walkers to competitive harriers. We meet through winter (April–October) and offer pack runs so you can go at your own pace.

### 2. Is this for me?
- ✅ New to running — can you walk/jog ~30 minutes? You’re welcome.
- ✅ Walkers — dedicated packs.
- ✅ Social runners — recreational membership, no pressure to race.
- ✅ Families — juniors from age 5.
- ⚠️ Hills — many runs use Johnsonville trails; we run in all weather (bring a layer).

### 3. Try before you join
- Come to **2–3 Saturday afternoon club runs** before registering (no fee).
- **No race bib or club kit needed** for trial visits.
- Check the [calendar](https://www.olympicharriers.nz/listcalendar/) for **“Club Run/Walk”** — best days for first-timers.

### 4. This week’s best session for newcomers
- **Next Club Run/Walk:** [DATE], ~1:30 pm, **Waiora Hub, top field, Alex Moore Park, Bannister Avenue, Johnsonville** *(verify time with captain)*.
- On **race weeks** we may be at a race venue — [link to explainer FAQ] or email the captain before travelling.

### 5. What to bring / expect
- Running shoes with grip; jacket; water.
- Arrive 10–15 minutes early.
- Short briefing, then out in pace groups; back to clubrooms for chat.
- Typical social run: allow 45–75 minutes depending on pack.

### 6. Membership options and costs

### 3. FAQ — *“I’m slow and nervous — will I fit in?”*

> **Yes.** Most of us aren't elite athletes — many members join to get fit, meet people, and enjoy a shared walk or jog. We run in **packs by pace**, including walkers and slower joggers, so you won't be left behind.  
>  
> You don't need to register before your first visit. Come to a **Club Run/Walk** Saturday (check our calendar), arrive a few minutes early, and tell the club captain you're new. A **recreational membership ($82/year)** covers social runs and club events if you decide to join — you don't have to enter races.  
>  
> Our routes can include hills around Johnsonville, so wear grippy shoes and a wind layer. If you're unsure which Saturday to pick, [contact the club captain](https://www.olympicharriers.nz/contact/) — we're happy to help.

## How to measure success

Track quarterly — simple counts a volunteer can maintain:

| Indicator | How to measure | Target (6 months) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **First-visit enquiries** | Captain/registrar logs emails/form messages mentioning “new” / “first time” / “try” | Increase vs prior season baseline |
| **Trial-to-join conversion** | Registrar asks new recreational members: *“How many Saturdays did you attend before joining?”* | Majority report 2+ trial visits |
| **Recreational vs competitive split** | Count new recreational ($82) sign-ups vs competitive | Recreational share increases among adult newcomers |
| **New Members page traffic** | Webmaster checks page views if analytics available; else track link clicks from Facebook | Steady growth through winter season |
| **Social post engagement** | Likes/comments on beginner posts vs results posts | Beginner posts match or exceed results posts |

**Qualitative check:** Ask one new recreational member each month: *“What almost stopped you from coming? What helped most?”* — feed answers back into the New Members page.
