Common questions

I'm slow and nervous — will I fit in?

Yes. Most of us aren't elite athletes — many members join to get fit, meet people, and enjoy a shared walk or jog. We run in packs by pace, including walkers and slower joggers, so you won't be left behind.

You don't need to register before your first visit. Come to a Club Run/Walk Saturday, arrive a few minutes early, and tell the club captain you're new. Our routes can include hills around Johnsonville — wear grippy shoes and a wind layer.

Contact the club captain if you're unsure which Saturday to pick — we're happy to help.
